    The birth chart, or natal chart, is where it all begins-
a snapshot that shows us the positions of all planets and asteriods from the moment you entered Earth.
    
    what does this have to do with you?
    
    To put it simply: energy doesn't lie. Each planet is representative of certain energies and themes we experience through our lives.
Through the understanding of the position of the stars in relation to your birth, we are able to calculate life direction, temperment, desire, needs, and even deepest wounds.
A common misunderstanding is that astrology defines you, it doesn't.
What it can do is tell you what themes you feel more comfortable in, as well as what qualities you need to develop within yourself during your journey in this life.

    Why does a leo moon crave recognition and appreciation? Why does a pisces sun sacrifice for love?
    Why does a scorpio venus keep their cards so close to their chest? Questions like this are explained easily with the help of natal chart readings.

Aries energy is not always chaotic, their desire is to be fun, free, and independent.
Taurus energy is not addicted to comfort, their desire is to experience the pleasure and beauty of life.
Gemini energy is endlessly curious, extremely complex, leading them into many rapidly changing life experiences.
Scorpio energy often scans for betrayal because, for them, everything really is that deep, even small things have meaning.
 
    And so on-

Now consider the energy of the planets, just like the signs they each embody themes such as rebellion, societal change, luck, etc.
The natal chart is important because we are able to read these energies in context.
The understanding of how these symbols interact in relation to each other gives us insight into the unseen. The cosmic wink.

    Even so, this is only the beginning.

Add in asteroids, nodes of the moon, dispositor trees, critical degrees, etc. and suddenly the energy around you, becomes more clear.
But why explore this? These energies and transits influence your life whether they are acknowledged or not.
Once you become aware, you can move with the ebbs and flows of life, rather than letting it take you for a ride; Soon you are swimming with the waves, not against them.
